BuildCraft Guide is an offline-first Android app. It does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data.

No accountNo adsNo trackingNo internet permission

About this policy

Effective date: August 29, 2026

This policy applies to BuildCraft Guide version 1.3.0, an informational Android application associated with company information supplied for INNOVA BTP.

Data collection and sharing

The app does not collect or share personal data. It has no account system, contact form, analytics, advertising, tracking, crash-reporting service, remote database or advertising identifier integration.

Information stored on the device

The app can store saved topics, preparation-checklist choices and an optional project note in local app preferences. This information stays on the device and is not transmitted.

Users can remove saved topics, clear the checklist and note in the app, or erase all local app information by clearing app data or uninstalling the app.

Offline tools

The length converter, area calculator, volume calculator and paint coverage estimator process entered numbers in memory. Local search text, calculator values and temporary sketches are not saved or transmitted.

Permissions and network access

The app requests no Android permissions, including no Internet permission, and makes no network requests.

Optional external-app actions

If the user taps a maps or sharing button, Android asks a compatible installed app to handle the supplied public business information. BuildCraft Guide does not receive information back. The selected third-party app operates under its own privacy practices.

Children and sensitive information

The app is a general business-information and educational utility. It is not directed specifically at children and does not request personal or sensitive information.

Policy changes

This policy will be reviewed when the app's code, dependencies or data practices change. A revision will show a new effective date.
